Design System · Listings Page · Rebrand Options

Listings Page Rebrand
— Three Approaches

All three approaches keep every existing filter, sort, view-toggle, and listing card functional. Only the visual chrome is changed to align with the lead-management Forest/Parchment brand language.

The Brand Gap — What We're Fixing

#0C0D10 → #1A3C28 Search / header zone background changes from Carbon dark to Forest green — matching Lead Management, KYC, and all other authenticated screens.
No header → Forest header Add a proper page-header section with Fraunces title, IBM Plex Mono eyebrow, and subtitle — consistent with every other page in the app.
Amber btn → Ghost btn AI Search button changes from standalone amber to ghost style, keeping terracotta as the sole primary call-to-action colour (Search button).
3 options below → Your choice Three approaches differ in how much vertical space the header takes and where the search/filter chrome sits. Functionality is identical across all three.

Philosophy

Full parity with Lead Management. Forest header + KPI strip + tab bar = one consistent authenticated shell across the entire app.

Effort

Minimal. Replace the #0C0D10 wrapper with forest and add the header block. No filter logic changes.

Best For

Users who navigate between Listings and Lead Management — immediate continuity. No visual jarring.

What Changed

The dark carbon search block is replaced with the Forest green header zone — identical structure to Lead Management, Construction Dashboard, and the Agent Cockpit. KPI strip and tab bar added above search filters.

What Stays the Same

All filter dropdowns, listing cards, sort controls, view-mode toggle (grid/list/map), AI voice search, and multi-listing drawer are untouched. Zero functional change.

Token Changes Only

#0C0D10 → #1A3C28 for the wrapper background. #101518 → #162E1E for dropdown panels.

AI Search Button

Amber colour removed. AI Search moves to a ghost-style button matching the btn-ghost pattern everywhere else. The terracotta Search CTA remains the sole strong call-to-action.

Philosophy

Forest header for identity, white card for search. The search form floats above the parchment body, giving a premium property-portal feel.

Effort

Moderate. The search row and filter bar move into a white card that partially overlaps the forest zone. Requires minor layout restructuring.

Best For

When you want the Listings page to feel more like a property portal (Rightmove / Property24 premium) while still carrying the brand.

The "Float" Effect

The white search card overlaps the edge between forest and parchment by ~20px (translateY: -20px). It reads like the search is the focus, elevated above both context layers.

Category Tabs Move

For Sale / To Rent / Sold Prices tabs move from the bottom of the forest header into the top of the floating card. Slightly different hierarchy from Lead Management but feels natural.

Input Style Change

Inputs become white with forest-green borders instead of forest-tinted backgrounds. Higher contrast on the white card. Dropdowns remain dark (#101518) to anchor them visually.

Tradeoff

Slightly more vertical height due to the overlap zone. Works best with a wider viewport. The filter chips row is more discoverable here vs. squeezed in the forest row.

Philosophy

Slimmer forest presence. Compact hero accent with title + 2 stats, then a sticky white search strip. Feels like a modern property portal (Property24 / Rightmove).

Effort

Most refactoring. Sticky search strip requires scroll behaviour. The hero section is separate from the search row.

Best For

Maximising property card visible area on load. Great for mobile-first use where vertical space is precious. Cleanest search UX.

Sticky Search on Scroll

The parchment search strip becomes sticky as you scroll past the hero. The category pills stay visible (on the forest hero edge) only on initial load. This maximises card discovery above the fold.

Compact Hero

The forest zone is ~120px tall vs ~260px for Options A/B. Great for users who know what they want and want cards immediately. Could feel "thin" to first-time users who need orientation.

Mobile First

The filter pills collapse cleanly behind a "More" button on small screens. The sticky strip naturally becomes the persistent mobile filter bar without extra engineering.

Least Brand Depth

Still forest + parchment, but the brand presence is slimmer. May feel less "premium trust platform" and more "property search tool". Better fit if the Listings page is primarily a public portal rather than an agent cockpit.

Option A — Recommended

  • Full Visual Consistency with all other authenticated pages
  • KPI strip gives agents instant context (listings, avg price, verified %)
  • Category tabs in forest header — familiar from Lead Management
  • Smallest code change — mostly token swap
  • Best for: Agent-facing dashboard use

Option B — Premium Portal

  • More "property marketplace" look and feel
  • Floating card elevates the search CTA visually
  • Filter chips more visible on white background
  • Slightly more refactoring (translateY + card wrapper)
  • Best for: Buyer-facing marketplace feel

Option C — Space-Efficient

  • Maximum card density above the fold
  • Sticky search strip is mobile-natural
  • Most engineering effort (sticky scroll behaviour)
  • Thinner brand presence vs A and B
  • Best for: Mobile + public listing portal use
