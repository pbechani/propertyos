✦ Design Notes — What Changed & Why

Forest Brand Header

Same #1A3C28 forest green hero header from My Listings, including the decorative terracotta + electric-green circles, Fraunces display title, and IBM Plex Mono eyebrow/KPI labels.

Pipeline Stage Track

Each card shows a live progress bar (stage X / 14) with stage name. Replaces the generic "Stage X / 15" text. Makes progress tactile and scannable at a glance.

Left Accent Bar

Cards use a 3px coloured left border — green for active, grey for completed, red for disputed — matching the listing card heat treatment approach in My Listings.

Smart Alerts Bar

Ported from My Listings. Shows actionable pipeline blockers (docs missing, deeds pending) rather than generic "you have N sales".

Fraunces Price Display

Agreed purchase price rendered in Fraunces serif — the same treatment as portfolio value in My Listings — giving financial figures appropriate visual weight.

Forest Modal Header

The "Initiate Sale" modal now opens with a dark forest header matching the page header. Labels, inputs, and selects inherit the brand border/focus tokens.

Before (Current)

− bg-gray-50 generic background
− Blue accent (text-blue-600) unrelated to brand
− Default Tailwind/Inter typography
− "Stage X / 15" plain text
− Simple 3-stat row (no KPI strip)
− No smart alerts
− Generic modal with white header

After (Redesign)

+ Forest #1A3C28 hero header + parchment bg
+ Terracotta CTAs, electric-green data, brand tokens
+ Fraunces (display) + IBM Plex Mono (labels)
+ Visual stage progress bar per card
+ 5-cell KPI strip (pipeline value, avg stage…)
+ Smart pipeline alerts (docs missing, deeds pending)
+ Forest-header modal matching brand
