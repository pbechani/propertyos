Design Decisions & Recommendations

1
3-way type selector (tabbed cards) — Lead / Client / Ad-hoc each shown as a tappable card with icon + description. The active type is visually raised with a green check. Instantly scannable and works on mobile too.
2
Live search with inline dropdown — Searches against /leads or /users/search depending on type. Shows avatar initials, name, email, phone and temperature badge (🔥 Hot / ⚡ Warm) so the agent can identify the right record at a glance.
3
Auto-fill with visual confirmation — Once a lead/client is selected, email and phone auto-populate with a green "From lead record" indicator. Fields are read-only but editable on click (override mode) for one-off corrections without touching the CRM.
4
Ad-hoc CRM prompt — When type is Ad-hoc, a contextual nudge offers to save the contact as a lead after booking. One checkbox — low friction, high CRM hygiene upside.
5
Reusable <PersonSelector> component — The type picker + search + auto-fill block will be extracted as a single component accepting onSelect(person) + mode prop. Ready for re-use on Open House check-in, Offer submission, Communication logs, and any future CRM touchpoint.
6
API wiring plan — Lead search → leadsApi.list({ search }). Client search → usersApi.search(). Viewing submit → viewingsApi.bookForBuyer() extended to accept leadId and personType. No new endpoints required for MVP; just extend payload.
