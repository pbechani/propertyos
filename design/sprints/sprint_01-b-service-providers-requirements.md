Service Providers

	•	Service providers can be a company or and individual sole proprietor 
	•	A company will have admins
	•	An admin is a user in the app and will register as type business partner and add company profile data
	•	The company profile will have to be verified
	•	Once that is done, the admin can create company users in the system and an email invitation is sent to the created user and to accept and create their profile with the role assigned to them and that profile is then linked to the company under the category the company operates
	•	The admin can give permission to the user on what they are allowed to do under the company (e.g create listings if its an agent)
	•	A user can belong to multiple companies
	•	When a user logs in, if they belong to multiple roles and companies, they should be asked which company & role they want to login to
	•	Everything/transaction that the company user does after login is registered or logged under that company
	•	The company admin will have visibility of everything the user is doing under the company and can escalate certain task to other users
	•	A company can have multiple admins and an admin should be able to create new company admins
	•	The company admin can revoke privileges of a user under that company
	•	When the user’ privileges are revoked, the user cannot login to that company, all the user’s activities/tasks are put in a special pool for that company where they can be reallocated. If communication needs to be sent to affected parties, the system should highlight that
