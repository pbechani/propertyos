About This Property

Nestled in the prestigious enclave of Bishopscourt, this executive family home offers refined living across 320m² of thoughtfully designed space. Floor-to-ceiling glazing frames Table Mountain views from the living room, while the wraparound entertainer's deck overlooks an indigenous garden and heated pool. The home is fully alarmed, walled, and offers solar-backup power — ideal for both local and diaspora buyers seeking a turnkey lifestyle asset.

An erf of 865m² provides generous outdoor space. The kitchen features Miele appliances, Caesarstone counters, and an integrated scullery. The master suite commands mountain views with a walk-in wardrobe and en-suite spa bathroom.
