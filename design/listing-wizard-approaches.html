New Listing Screen · Design Decision

Three Approaches to
Unified Listing Creation

All three use the same PRIBEC brand tokens, Fraunces headings, and parchment palette.
The choice is about UX pattern, not branding — every option looks on-brand.

Low complexity

Evolved Tabs
