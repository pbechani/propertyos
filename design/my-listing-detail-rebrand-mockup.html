Description

This immaculate 4-bedroom family home is nestled in the prestigious Morningside extension, offering an exceptional blend of modern luxury and timeless elegance. The home features an open-plan kitchen and living area that flows seamlessly onto a covered entertainer's patio overlooking the sparkling pool. The chef's kitchen is fitted with Caesarstone counters, a 5-burner gas hob, and integrated appliances. The master suite boasts a walk-in dressing room and spa-style en-suite bathroom. Three additional bedrooms all have built-in cupboards, with two sharing a family bathroom. The double automated garage leads directly into the home with additional covered parking. Security features include an 8-camera CCTV system, electric fencing, and 24-hour estate guarding.

Proposed Enhancements
6 new capabilities incorporated into this design
01 — HERO
Immersive Hero Panel
Full-bleed property image with a forest-green info panel fused to the right. Key price, specs, days on market, and portal views are all visible above the fold — no scrolling required. Eliminates the thin topbar-only pattern.
New layout
02 — ACTIVITY
Real-Time Activity Pulse Strip
A sticky alert row beneath the hero shows live counts for viewing requests, enquiries, and offers with colour-coded chips. Clicking a chip deep-links directly to that tab — replaces the ActivitySummary whitebox with something far more prominent.
New component
03 — FINANCE
Price Intelligence Row
The financial card now shows a "+2.3% vs market" badge derived from comparable sales. Asking price is displayed alongside a per-m² rate so agents can justify pricing to buyers on the spot.
New data point
04 — SIDEBAR
Performance Mini-Bar Chart
A sidebar card with horizon bars for Views, Enquiries, Viewings, and Offers in the last 30 days. Gives agents a quick health-check on listing performance without leaving the details tab.
New component
05 — ACTIONS
Consolidated Quick Actions Panel
All 8 common agent actions (schedule viewing, add offer, upload doc, share listing, generate report…) are grouped in a persistent sidebar card with colour-coded icons. Reduces click-depth for the most frequent tasks.
New component
06 — TOKENS
Refined Design System
Fraunces (display) + DM Sans (body) + JetBrains Mono (labels/refs). Parchment backgrounds with forest green as the dominant accent, terra red and amber gold as secondary signals. Consistent border-radius scale (8/14/20/28px).
